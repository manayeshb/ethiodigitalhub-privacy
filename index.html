Privacy Policy: EthioDigital HUB

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Developer: Manayesh Belay Nega

Contact: manibelay88@gmail.com

1. About the app

EthioDigital HUB helps you discover, browse, search and save links to AI tools and online-income resources. It includes an in-app assistant that suggests tools from the app's own directory, and a Daily Gift feature that unlocks free guides.

2. Information we do not ask for

The app has no accounts and no login. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following on your device only: language and theme, favorites and saved lists, recent searches, Daily Gift progress (the number of days you opened the Gift and the date of the last opening), tip position, and whether you have seen the welcome screen. This data is not sent to us. You can remove it by clearing the app's data in your device settings. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, your Gift progress is lost.

4. The assistant and search

Your questions and search terms are processed on your device only, using the tool data included in the app. They are not sent to any server.

5. Internet use

To show tool logos, the app requests icons from the third-party service icon.horse, using the website address of each tool displayed. That service receives technical information such as your IP address and standard request details, and is governed by its own privacy policy. We do not receive or store this information. Logos may be cached on your device after they load.

6. External links and contact

Tool links open third-party websites in your browser. The "Contact us" button opens your email app so you can write to us.

We are not affiliated with the listed tools and are not responsible for their content, prices, availability or privacy practices. Free plans and limits change, so check each tool's website.

7. Advertising and analytics

The app does not show ads. It does not use analytics, crash reporting or advertising identifiers.

8. Children

The app is for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it with third parties.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top will change when we do.
